# Comparative Positioning

This document explains how ValoraBTC differs from common BTC-to-DeFi approaches.

---

## What ValoraBTC Is Not

ValoraBTC is **not**:

- A wrapped BTC clone (e.g. simple IOU tokens)
- A Bitcoin Layer 2
- A yield-maximization platform
- A custodial BTC product

---

## Core Difference: Coordination vs Custody

Most BTC DeFi solutions require users to commit to a single trust model:
- Custodian
- Bridge
- Multi-sig signer set
- Dedicated L2 environment

ValoraBTC is designed as a **liquidity routing & settlement coordination layer**, enabling interaction across BTC liquidity rails without enforcing a single custody architecture.
