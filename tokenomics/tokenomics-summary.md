# ValoraBTC Tokenomics Summary

This document provides a high-level, non-marketing overview of the ValoraBTC dual-token economic model.  
For full context and rationale, refer to the official whitepaper.

---

## Token Overview

ValoraBTC uses a **dual-token architecture** to clearly separate economic value from protocol mechanics.

### VLBTC — Ecosystem & Value Token

- **Total Supply:** 21,000,000 (fixed, no inflation)
- **Supply Model:** Hard-capped at genesis
- **Primary Roles:**
  - Presale participation
  - Ecosystem incentives
  - Staking and participation rewards
  - Governance signaling (future phases)

VLBTC is the **only market-facing token** of the ValoraBTC ecosystem.

---

### VLCOR — Technical Coordination Token

- **Supply Model:** Dynamic (minted and burned in relation to BTC flows)
- **Role:** Internal protocol coordination and settlement logic
- **Audience:** Validators, protocol infrastructure, system-level operations
- **Market Status:** Not marketed, not speculative

VLCOR exists solely to support BTC liquidity routing and settlement mechanics.  
It is **not designed as an investment asset**.

---

## Allocation Principles (VLBTC)

Rather than aggressive emissions, ValoraBTC follows a **conservative distribution philosophy**:

- No inflation-based rewards
- No unsustainable APY mechanics
- No opaque rebase or supply expansion logic

Typical allocation categories include:
- Presale participants
- Ecosystem development
- Liquidity provisioning
- Strategic reserves
- Governance and long-term protocol sustainability

Exact percentages and vesting schedules are defined in the whitepaper.

---

## Vesting & Distribution Discipline

- No immediate token transfers post-purchase
- Tokens are claimable at Token Generation Event (TGE)
- Vesting applies to non-public allocations
- Liquidity provisioning follows controlled, transparent rules
- LP tokens are locked

This approach prioritizes **market stability and long-term alignment** over short-term speculation.

---

## Design Philosophy

ValoraBTC tokenomics are intentionally designed to:
- Avoid inflationary pressure
- Minimize reflexive speculation loops
- Align incentives with real protocol usage
- Support sustainable BTC liquidity participation
