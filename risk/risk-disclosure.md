# Risk Disclosure

This document outlines key risks associated with the ValoraBTC Protocol.  
It is provided for transparency and informed evaluation.

---

## General Disclaimer

ValoraBTC is an experimental protocol.  
Participation involves risk, including but not limited to partial or total loss of funds.

This document does not constitute financial advice.

---

## Protocol Design Risks

- Smart contract vulnerabilities may exist despite audits and reviews
- Routing and settlement logic may behave unexpectedly under edge cases
- Dependency on external infrastructure introduces non-deterministic risks

---

## BTC Liquidity Risks

- BTC routing mechanisms may rely on third-party bridges or custody models
- Changes in BTC network conditions can affect settlement timing
- External failures may impact protocol functionality

ValoraBTC focuses on **coordination**, not custody, but systemic risks remain.

---

## Economic & Market Risks

- VLBTC price may be volatile or decline significantly
- No guarantees are made regarding liquidity depth or market demand
- Protocol adoption may not meet expectations

---

## Regulatory & Compliance Risks

- Regulatory treatment of crypto assets varies by jurisdiction
- Future regulations may affect token usability, trading, or protocol operation
- Participants are responsible for local compliance

---

## Operational Risks

- Development delays are possible
- Feature scope may change based on security or regulatory concerns
- Governance decisions may evolve over time

---

## No Yield Guarantees

- No fixed or guaranteed returns are offered
- No unsustainable APYs are promised
- Rewards depend on protocol activity and participation

---

## User Responsibility

Participants are expected to:
- Read the full whitepaper
- Understand token roles (VLBTC vs VLCOR)
- Assess personal risk tolerance

---

## Final Note

Explicit risk disclosure is a deliberate design choice.  
ValoraBTC prioritizes clarity, sustainability, and long-term protocol integrity over short-term hype.
